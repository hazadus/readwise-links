    > **Резюме:** Rules in Cursor provide a way to give consistent guidance to the AI by encoding context and preferences for projects. There are three types of rules: Project Rules, which are version-controlled and specific to a codebase; User Rules, which apply globally; and a deprecated format called .cursorrules. These rules help automate workflows and standardize decisions, ensuring the AI generates responses with relevant context.
